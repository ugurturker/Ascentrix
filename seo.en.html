T-Peak Focus Protocol

Ascentrix: The Pomodoro Timer That Adapts to Your Real Focus Capacity

Instead of forcing the same 25 minutes on you every day, Ascentrix measures how long you can genuinely concentrate. That measured T-Peak value becomes the basis for your focus steps, laid out on a 1.00T → 0.80T → 0.65T → 0.50T ladder. Every step is a little shorter than the one before it: your freshest, most productive slice of the day gets the longest step, and the part where you are running on empty gets the shortest one.

How It Works

Build a focus protocol of your own in three steps. No setup, no mandatory account and no ready-made templates — everything is derived from data you actually produce.

1.00T
Full

The top of the ladder. Your measured T-Peak is used in full here — this is your freshest, sharpest slice of the day.

0.80T
Friction

Focus is still running, but the friction of the work itself starts to bite. A shorter block makes the work more deliberate.

0.65T
Fatigue

Energy has clearly dropped. Here it pays off more to finish the goal than to extend the block.

0.50T
Descent

The floor and the bottom of the descent. After this comes a break: banking the remaining energy beats spending it.

So how is T found? Start the timer, genuinely focus, and press FINISH the moment you lose interest. The time that has elapsed becomes your T-Peak; measurements accumulate throughout the day and are charted over time. Every time you beat your record, a new T-Peak entry is created.

Why does every step get a little shorter? Not by an arbitrary rule, but by the real fatigue curve measured in a single session. As you keep working on the same thing, focus resistance rises on its own — so the steps narrow progressively and the floor is pinned at 0.50T. The four steps add up to 2.95×T: with a 20-minute T-Peak, one full cycle is roughly 59 minutes. Break length follows a separate rhythm — see below.

Privacy

Ascentrix's data model is simple, and you stay in control of it.

Your account only

Data is kept solely in the Firestore document attached to your own Google account. No selling of cross-account data, no advertising profiles, no third-party tracking.

Signing in is optional

You can use the app without signing in; in that case data is limited to the open session and is gone once you close the tab.

No community tracking

No ad network, no analytics SDK, no cookie-based profiling and no session recording. Your statistics are shown to you and nobody else.

Your data is yours

All your progress and history can be deleted at any time. Removing the account also clears the Firestore record.

Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask about Ascentrix most often.

Is Ascentrix free?

Yes. Ascentrix is completely free and ad-free. There is no premium tier, no subscription, no trial and no in-app purchase. If you would like to support the project, you can optionally contribute via Patreon.

What is T-Peak and how is it measured?

T-Peak is the longest span during which you stayed genuinely productive that day. Ascentrix counts up while you focus. The moment you press FINISH because you lost focus, that elapsed time is recorded as your T-Peak, and your whole daily focus plan is derived from it.

How is this different from a classic pomodoro timer?

A classic pomodoro applies the same fixed 25/5 block to everyone. Ascentrix measures your capacity instead of assuming it, shortens focus steps according to your real concentration level, and applies a break ratio of your choice rather than the scientific default of 25%. You follow a decaying focus curve instead of repeating identical blocks.

Where is my data stored?

Your data lives only in the Firebase Firestore document tied to your own Google account. If you do not sign in, data is limited to the current session and disappears when you close the browser. Ascentrix uses no ad network, no analytics tracker and no third-party profiling.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Ascentrix is a PWA, so after the first load it works without an internet connection. On mobile you can add it to your home screen, on desktop to your taskbar.

Which devices are supported?

It runs on any device with a modern browser: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. When you sign in with Google, your data syncs across all of them automatically.
